# वेणु · Venu

A quiet journey into Krishna's flute.

Scroll through five short chapters (the lips, the bamboo, the openings, the knot and the peacock feather), each one with
a verse from the Veṇu-gīta. Turn the flute with your finger, zoom in to see the carving, and let the music play.

**See it here:** https://project-venu-opal.vercel.app

Best with sound on, and with the phone or screen brightness turned up a little.
