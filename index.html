वेणु

Venu

the flute of the divine

chapter i

Adhara Sudhā

अधरसुधा — the nectar of the lips

Where breath enters bamboo and emerges as song. The player's lips rest at this threshold — the narrow gate between silence and sound, where the finite meets the infinite.

chapter ii

Vansha

वंश — the bamboo

The reed must be hollowed to sing. Emptied of pith, freed of self — only then can the divine breath flow through it. The grain remembers the forest; the hollow remembers surrender.

chapter iii

Randhra

रन्ध्र — the openings

Six wounds drilled through the body. Each one a doorway for a different prayer. The player's fingers dance — opening, closing — and the bamboo speaks in voices it never knew it held.

chapter iv

Granthi

ग्रन्थि — the knot

Where the bamboo once branched, a ring remains. The knots mark seasons of growth — memories that hollowing could not erase. Proof that even a vessel of the divine once reached for its own light.

chapter v

Mayūra Piccha

मयूरपिच्छ — the peacock plume

His mark upon it. A feather set in gold, with silk cords hanging beside it — it says: this is not merely an instrument. It belongs to the one whose music breaks the rivers’ rush and sets the stars to dance.

गोप्यः किमाचरदयं कुशलं स्म वेणुर्दामोदराधरसुधामपि गोपिकानाम्

gopyaḥ kim ācarad ayaṁ kuśalaṁ sma veṇur dāmodarādhara-sudhām api gopikānām

what good deed did this flute perform, that it alone enjoys the nectar of Dāmodara’s lips — meant for the gopīs?

Śrīmad Bhāgavatam 10.21.9

भुङ्क्ते स्वयं यदवशिष्टरसं ह्रदिन्यो हृष्यत्त्वचोऽश्रु मुमुचुस्तरवो यथार्याः

bhuṅkte svayaṁ yad avaśiṣṭa-rasaṁ hradinyo hṛṣyat-tvaco ’śru mumucus taravo yathāryāḥ

it drinks it all itself, leaving only a remnant — and the rivers shiver with joy, and the trees, like elders of its line, weep tears of delight

Śrīmad Bhāgavatam 10.21.9

रन्ध्रान् वेणोरधरसुधयापूरयन्

randhrān veṇor adhara-sudhayāpūrayan

filling the hollows of the flute with the nectar of his lips

Śrīmad Bhāgavatam 10.21.5

गावश्च कृष्णमुखनिर्गतवेणुगीतपीयूषम्

gāvaś ca kṛṣṇa-mukha-nirgata-veṇu-gīta-pīyūṣam

the cows, ears raised like cups, drink the nectar of the flute-song flowing from Kṛṣṇa’s mouth

Śrīmad Bhāgavatam 10.21.13

बर्हापीडं नटवरवपुः कर्णयोः कर्णिकारं

barhāpīḍaṁ naṭa-vara-vapuḥ karṇayoḥ karṇikāraṁ

a peacock plume upon his crown, karṇikāra blossoms at his ears — the finest of dancers

Śrīmad Bhāgavatam 10.21.5

जय श्री कृष्ण

jaya śrī kṛṣṇa

नादब्रह्म

nādabrahma — sound is the divine

When the last note fades,
do not search for it.
The silence that follows
is also His song.
